🔐 WhoHasAccess

Privacy policy

Effective 2 November 2026

The short version. WhoHasAccess runs entirely in your browser. It reads the names and sharing settings of your Google Drive files straight from Google, shows you who has access, and changes sharing only when you click. There is no server, no database, no account and no analytics. Nothing about your files is sent anywhere except to Google's own APIs.

What WhoHasAccess is

WhoHasAccess is an open-source web app that audits the sharing settings of your Google Drive: who can open your files, which links are public, which shares are stale, and how to fix them. It is a static web page; the host that serves it only ever sees requests for the page and its script and style files.

Data we access

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for these permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive: to list your files' metadata (name, type, folder, dates, owner) and their permissions, and to change those permissions when you ask it to. Drive offers no narrower permission that can remove or change a share.
  • userinfo.email and userinfo.profile: to show who is connected and to tell your own access apart from everyone else's.
  • drive.activity.readonly (optional): only if you click Load history on a file, to show its sharing timeline.

File contents are never requested, downloaded or exported. The "looks sensitive" hint is computed from the file name only, with a short list of patterns such as "passport" or "invoice".

Where processing happens

In your browser, and nowhere else. The app has no backend. Your Google access token is kept in the page's memory only and is gone when the tab closes. The only third-party script is Google Identity Services, which draws the Google sign-in. There are no analytics, trackers or advertising.

What we share

Nothing. No data about you or your files is sent to us or to anyone else. The browser talks only to Google's services: sign-in (accounts.google.com), the Drive API and, if you choose, the Drive Activity API. We do not sell, rent or transfer Google user data, and we do not use it for advertising or to train any model.

Changes the app makes to your Drive

Only on your click, and for bulk actions only after a confirm step. The possible changes are: remove a share, downgrade it to viewer, set it to expire in 30 days, make a public link unsearchable, transfer ownership (or send an ownership invite, cancel one or accept one). Each change is a direct call from your browser to the Drive API. Most changes offer Undo, which restores the share without emailing anyone.

Google API Services User Data Policy

WhoHasAccess's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Use Forget & disconnect in the app's Privacy menu, which revokes the token with Google and clears everything listed above. You can also remove WhoHasAccess at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page.
